# UNSAID — product case study

## Why this exists

Some words are easier to write than to say. UNSAID explores a quiet alternative to products that require social participation, emotional classification or consistent engagement. Intended users are adults who want a self-directed place to express themselves. This is a design premise, not a finding from invented interviews.

## Privacy as a product decision

No accounts and no cloud writing database reduce disclosure pathways and infrastructure. They also move responsibility for retention to the browser. Phase 2 makes that trade-off visible: local storage is plaintext, not a privacy vault. Encryption and recovery constraints must be addressed before sensitive use.

## Why no AI or social layer

The MVP uses no AI. Future reflection uses optional static prompts; any later AI proposal requires explicit opt-in and privacy review. An audience, reactions and sharing would change the character of the space, so social features are excluded. Letters will never be delivered.

## Thought Constellation

The planned graph represents saved writing as stars with user-controlled positions and manually created connections. It must offer an accessible list alternative and never interpret relationships as diagnoses or inferred psychological patterns. The foundation includes only a clearly marked preview.

## Technical trade-offs

IndexedDB supports asynchronous structured local persistence. A single draft keeps the first version understandable but limits parallel writing. BrowserRouter supports familiar navigation but requires hosting fallback rules. Local system fonts and CSS/SVG atmosphere eliminate third-party font or image requests. Static reflection avoids shipping private text to inference services.

## Next opportunities

Build and review the encrypted vault, then add letters, accessible constellation exploration and voluntary reflection. Test with consenting users without collecting private writing. Assess accessibility, comprehension of storage risks and reliability before expanding scope. No clinical benefit or adoption outcomes are claimed.
